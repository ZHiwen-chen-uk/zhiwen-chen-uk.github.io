Featured Project

BSc Dissertation · Sep 2024 – May 2025

SENAS: A Cyclical Three-Phase Evolutionary Algorithm for Multi-Objective Neural Architecture Search

University of Birmingham · Supervisor Dr. Jizheng Wan · Inspector Prof. Russell Beale · Topic introduced by Dr. Miqing Li

SENAS is a cyclic three-phase evolutionary NAS algorithm: SOM-guided diversity exploration, dual-archive convergence–diversity co-evolution, and ε+-indicator-guided refinement. A lightweight Monte-Carlo hypervolume estimator handles phase transitions without computing exact HV.

Benchmarked against NSGA-II, MOEA/D, HypE, RVEA and a SOM-SEMO baseline on the C10/MOP suite (NAS-Bench-101 / 201, NATS-Bench, DARTS), 30 independent runs per task, fixed budget of 10,000 architecture queries.
